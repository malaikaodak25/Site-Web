Les meilleurs artist du temps

Vincent Van Gogh was a famous Dutch post-impressionist painter. His paintings are characterized by dramatic color, thick brushstrokes and heavy textures. Van Gogh’s paintings can be classified into two periods: the early (1880-1886) and the late (1887-90).
